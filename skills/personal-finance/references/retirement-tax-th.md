# Retirement and tax notes for BUU staff

Planning background for Claude. Rules and limits change: tell the user to confirm figures with
BUU HR (กองการเจ้าหน้าที่), the fund manager or the Revenue Department before acting.

## Retirement date

- Civil servants and university employees retire at the end of the fiscal year (30 September) in
  which they reach 60. Under Thai law a person reaches an age on the day before the birthday, so
  someone born on 1 October retires on 30 September of the year they turn 60; someone born on
  2 October or later retires the following 30 September.
- Settings!B6 computes this from the date of birth.

## Employment types

| Type | Retirement income | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ข้าราชการ | บำนาญ (monthly pension) or บำเหน็จ (lump sum), plus กบข. (GPF) savings | Pensioners keep government medical benefits (สิทธิเบิกจ่ายตรง) for themselves and eligible family |
| พนักงานมหาวิทยาลัย | กองทุนสำรองเลี้ยงชีพ (provident fund: employee + employer contributions), social security pension from 55 if contributed 180+ months | No government pension |
| Transferred from ข้าราชการ | May receive a fixed lifetime pension already, plus provident fund | Ask what the "pension" line on the payslip is |

## Provident fund (PVD)

- Payouts on leaving are tax-free when the member is 55+ and has 5+ years of membership; leaving
  earlier (for example when changing jobs) can make them taxable unless moved to the new employer's
  fund or to an RMF for PVD.
- Employee contributions are tax-deductible (the first ฿10,000 as an allowance, the rest as
  exempt income), within the combined retirement-savings cap. Check that the return claims the full
  amount, not just ฿10,000.

## Tax-advantaged products (check current-year limits)

- RMF: deductible within a share of income and the combined retirement cap (about ฿500,000 with
  PVD, annuity insurance and others). Redeem tax-free at 55+ after 5 years of holding.
- ThaiESG: a separate deduction with its own cap.
- Annuity insurance (ประกันบำนาญ): deductible within its own share-of-income limit and the combined
  cap; life insurance premiums have a separate ฿100,000 allowance that the annuity can use first.
- Mortgage interest: up to ฿100,000 a year.
- SSF: no longer open for new deductions; existing units keep their own holding rules.
- Purchases count for a tax year only if made by 31 December.

## Using the Retirement tab

- Needed at retirement = the assets required on 30 Sep of the last working year to pay the yearly
  shortfall (spending need less pension, annuity and other income) up to the plan age, at the
  investment return. Projected assets = today's investments and counted savings, plus
  contributions, plus returns.
- The biggest unknowns are usually the provident-fund balance and contributions; fill them before
  drawing conclusions.
- Levers to show: raising PVD contributions, RMF/ThaiESG purchases (which also cut tax), extra
  saving after the mortgage is paid off, consulting or other income after retirement, a lower
  spending target, a separate education fund for children's university costs.
